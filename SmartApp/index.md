# Smart App Organizer Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: November 18, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

1.1 "Smart App Organizer" (hereinafter referred to as "this App") is an application provided by KAI_Q Studio ("we," "us," or "our") that helps users organize applications on their own Android devices. Its features include application discovery, categorization, search, launching, usage reviews, storage information, organization reminders, and user-requested uninstall workflows. We are committed to protecting user privacy and minimizing the information processed for these purposes.

1.2 This Privacy Policy explains how Smart App Organizer accesses, processes, stores, protects, and, where applicable, transmits information when you use the App.

1.3 This Policy provides information about our data practices. Where a feature requires your permission or consent, we provide the relevant explanation and request it separately before accessing the information concerned.

1.4 If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact us at:

**Email: [contact@kaiqstudio.com](mailto:contact@kaiqstudio.com)**

1.5 Smart App Organizer is designed for personal application organization on a device that you own or are authorized to manage. Information available to the App depends on Android permissions, application visibility rules, device settings, and the operating system.

1.6 Smart App Organizer does not require users to register an account with us or provide payment card information to use its core organization features. Android usage analysis requires a separate Usage Access authorization that you control in system settings.

## 2. Information and Data We Process

2.1 Smart App Organizer follows the principles of data minimization, local-first processing, and privacy by design. Its application catalog, categories, usage history, storage summaries, and organization preferences are designed to remain on your device. Information may leave your device in the specific circumstances described in this Policy, including purchases, update checks, support requests, and actions you initiate through external services or file providers.

### Application Catalog and Organization

Smart App Organizer uses information made available by Android to identify applications that it can organize or launch. This may include application names, icons, package names, installation and update dates, system or user application status, and available launch information.

The App stores this information locally to display your application catalog, recommend categories, support search and sorting, and maintain your organization choices when applications are installed, updated, or removed.

### Application Usage and Storage Information

If you grant Usage Access, Smart App Organizer may query Android-provided application usage records and statistics. These records may include application identifiers, usage event types and timestamps, last recorded use, foreground usage duration, and information used to estimate usage frequency.

Where supported and permitted by Android, the App may also retrieve application storage statistics, including application size, data size, and cache size. These are size measurements provided by the operating system.

Usage records and storage measurements are processed locally. Historical availability and completeness vary by device and system behavior. The App records information about observation periods, missing data, and synchronization status so that it can distinguish supported findings from unavailable or incomplete information.

### Review Lists and Organization Reminders

Smart App Organizer analyzes available local records to help identify applications with no recently recorded use or little recorded use since installation. It also respects your choices to protect an application or keep it without reminders.

These findings help you review your own applications. They are based on available records and your settings, and you decide which applications to retain, reorganize, or uninstall.

2.2 The types of information or data that may be processed when you use Smart App Organizer include:

(i) **Application Catalog Information:** Application names, icons, package identifiers, available launch information, installation dates, update dates, application status, and catalog refresh information.

(ii) **Categories and Organization Preferences:** Built-in category associations, custom category names and icons, manual classification choices, display order, sorting preferences, pinned applications, and category modification information.

(iii) **Application Usage Information:** Available usage events and timestamps, last recorded use, foreground usage duration, locally calculated daily summaries, active days, and usage frequency estimates where supported by the available data.

(iv) **Observation and Data Quality Information:** Permission status, observation periods, last successful data refresh, gaps in available records, and information necessary to explain the reliability of a usage assessment.

(v) **Storage Information:** Android-provided application, data, and cache size measurements, the time of a measurement, and information indicating whether a measurement is available or may involve shared storage accounting.

(vi) **Protection and Review Settings:** Protected application flags, keep-without-reminders preferences, inactivity thresholds, notification preferences, reminder schedules, and reminder delivery status.

(vii) **App Preferences and Local Technical Information:** Language, appearance, onboarding preferences, usage history retention settings, tracking pause settings, and limited local error or diagnostic information needed to operate and troubleshoot the App.

(viii) **Backup and Restore Data:** Categories, application-to-category associations, custom ordering, protection preferences, and supported settings that you intentionally export or restore. Backup files may reveal application package names and your organization choices. The supported settings backup excludes purchase tokens, purchase ownership, raw usage events, usage history summaries, and storage measurements.

(ix) **Uninstall Workflow Information:** Applications you select for removal and the local status of a user-requested uninstall workflow, including whether an operation was completed, canceled, skipped, or unsuccessful.

(x) **Customer Service and Feedback Information:** Messages and any contact details you intentionally provide by email or through a Formspree-powered support form. Email is optional in the support form. Service providers may also process technical connection and anti-abuse information associated with the submission.

(xi) **Purchase Information:** If you choose to purchase the optional Smart App Organizer Pro upgrade, Google Play processes the payment on Android. The App may receive product identifiers, purchase tokens, transaction status, acknowledgment status, and related information needed to check, acknowledge, restore, and manage your Pro entitlement. We do not directly receive your payment card number or Google account password.

(xii) **Update Check Information:** The App platform and distribution channel, and version information where needed to check for updates. The endpoint and its hosting provider may receive ordinary request metadata, such as your IP address, request time, and HTTP headers. The installed application catalog and usage history are excluded from update requests.

2.3 Smart App Organizer may use certain device capabilities when necessary to provide its features:

(i) **Application Visibility:** Android application queries allow the App to discover supported applications and obtain the metadata needed to organize or launch them. The available list may exclude applications or profiles that Android does not make visible to this App.

(ii) **Usage Access:** You may grant this special access in Android settings to enable supported usage analysis and, where Android requires it, storage statistics for other applications. The App may periodically read available usage information in the background to maintain local history. Android controls when background work runs.

(iii) **Notifications:** If you enable organization reminders, the App may request notification permission on Android versions that require it. You can manage reminders in the App and notifications in system settings.

(iv) **File Access:** The App may access a file or destination that you explicitly select when creating a supported backup or restoring organization settings.

(v) **Uninstall Requests:** When you choose to remove another application, Smart App Organizer invokes the supported Android uninstall workflow. Android presents the required confirmation and controls the outcome.

(vi) **Internet Access:** Internet connectivity may be used for automatic update checks after App startup, manual update checks, purchase-related operations, and access to websites or support services. External links are opened through the device's supported browser or application.

2.4 You may decline or revoke Usage Access. Basic categorization, search, and launching remain available for applications visible to the App, while usage-dependent features may become unavailable or display incomplete information.

2.5 Smart App Organizer processes system-provided application metadata, usage information, and storage measurements. It does not read the private files, messages, passwords, or in-app content of other applications. Its core features do not require access to your contacts, microphone, camera, or precise location.

2.6 Smart App Organizer does not automatically upload your application catalog, classification choices, protection list, usage history, or storage summaries to servers operated by us. The App does not provide automatic cloud synchronization or remote AI analysis of this information.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Application catalog information, categories, usage history, storage summaries, protection preferences, and related settings are designed to be stored in the App's private storage on your device.

4.2 Smart App Organizer does not operate a remote account service for automatically storing or synchronizing your application catalog or usage history.

4.3 Local usage information is retained for the history period supported by the App and selected in its settings. Detailed recent events are retained only as needed to reconcile and summarize usage, after which they are removed under the App's retention rules. Changing the history period or clearing usage history affects which longer-term findings remain available.

4.4 Application organization preferences are retained while they are needed for your organization settings or until you reset or delete them. Storage measurements are refreshed or replaced as new measurements become available. Limited local diagnostic records are kept on a rotating basis for troubleshooting.

4.5 You can clear stored usage history, pause further usage collection, and reset supported organization information through the available controls. Revoking Usage Access stops future access to protected system usage information but does not by itself erase records already saved in the App. Those records can be removed separately through the App's data controls or Android settings.

4.6 The App provides user-initiated backup and restore functions for supported organization settings. Exported files are placed under your control through the operating system's file selection mechanisms.

4.7 A settings backup can contain application identifiers, category associations, and protection choices. Protect the file and select a storage destination appropriate to the sensitivity of that information. The App's supported backup format should be treated as readable by anyone who can access the exported file.

4.8 If you select a cloud drive or another external file provider as the backup destination, that provider may upload or synchronize the file according to its own settings and privacy practices. Exported files are not automatically uploaded to a backup service operated by us.

4.9 Uninstalling Smart App Organizer or clearing its storage through Android may remove locally saved organization information and usage history. Files exported outside the App, support correspondence, and transaction records held by an app store are managed separately and may remain until deleted or removed under the relevant provider's practices.

4.10 Android backup, device transfer, and restoration behavior can vary by operating system version, manufacturer, and user settings. Any copy or transfer performed by your operating system or selected storage provider is governed by its settings and applicable practices. We do not maintain a recoverable remote copy of your local usage history.

4.11 Purchase entitlement information is stored separately from organization backups. The App uses platform-supported protections for local purchase information and checks purchase status with Google Play when the relevant service is available.

4.12 Support messages and contact information under our control are retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry, address follow-up issues, and meet applicable legal or security obligations. We delete or de-identify information when it is no longer needed for those purposes. You may request deletion using the contact details in this Policy. Service providers apply their own retention requirements to information under their control.

4.13 Update hosting and support providers may retain connection or anti-abuse logs under their applicable service and security practices. These logs are separate from your local application usage history.

4.14 We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information and services involved, including the operating system's application isolation and encrypted HTTPS connections for App-controlled network requests. Security also depends on your device, operating system, and any external storage or services you choose to use.
