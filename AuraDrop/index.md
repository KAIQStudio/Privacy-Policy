## 2. Information We Collect and Dual-Track Data Policy
2.1 This App follows the principles of least privilege and privacy first, adopting independent local storage and encryption mechanisms. It primarily adopts the dual-track data processing policy of "positive energy = permanent collection; negative energy = instant release":

Positive Energy Records (Collection): The text and recordings (maximum 20 seconds, processed with high-efficiency audio compression) you enter in the "Positive Energy" module are securely stored in your device's local database. You may review, play back, or manually delete them at any time.

Negative Energy Records (Release): The text and recordings (maximum 20 seconds) you enter in the "Emotional Release Room" are used only to generate visual glass bottle particles and instant release animations (including voice frequency-shifting shatter and dissipation sound effects). The original text and original audio files are never permanently stored and are completely erased from the local device immediately after the release ritual is completed.

2.2 The specific types of information processed or collected are as follows:

(i) Text and Voice Data: The positive energy diary text you actively enter and the 20-second positive energy voice recordings you make, used only for the accumulation and historical review of the local "Energy World";

(ii) Device Information: This includes basic data such as operating system version and App version number, used to ensure the stable operation of the App and adapt to different devices;

(iii) Customer Service Message Information: When you use the customer service function through the settings page, the message content you enter will be transmitted through the third-party customer service provider Formspree, and we use it only to respond to your technical support requests.

2.3 This App may request access to the following device permissions, and you may choose whether to authorize them at your own discretion:

Microphone Permission: Used only for you to actively record positive or negative energy voice messages of up to 20 seconds; refusing authorization does not affect your use of the text recording function;

Storage/File Permission: Used only to export your positive energy data and voice backups as JSON/ZIP files or to perform backup restoration; refusing authorization does not affect the storage of data within the App.

2.4 All collection and processing of information is based on your explicit consent. You may clear local data at any time through device settings or options within the App.
