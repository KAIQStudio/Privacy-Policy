# Time Canvas Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: [Publication Date]**

## 1. Introduction

1.1 Time Canvas is a creative application for making and sharing time-limited canvases with drawings, text, photos, and supported audio or video. This Privacy Policy applies to the Time Canvas app and the browser-based canvas viewer.

1.2 This Policy explains what information we process, why we use it, who may receive it, and how you can make privacy choices or requests.

1.3 Please read this Policy before publishing a canvas. Where consent is required by applicable law, we will ask for it at the relevant step.

1.4 You may contact us by email if you have questions about this Policy.

**Email: [contact@kaiqstudio.com](mailto:contact@kaiqstudio.com)**

1.5 A viewer can open a valid canvas link in a supported browser without an app installation or viewer account. Anyone who obtains the valid link can use it according to the applicable viewing rules.

1.6 You can create and edit drafts locally. An internet connection is required to publish a canvas or view a published canvas. Publishing may require verification of the sender or their app installation for security and abuse prevention.

## 2. Information and Data We Process

2.1 Time Canvas keeps unpublished drafts and sent-history records on your device. When you choose to publish, the selected canvas and the information needed to manage its link are sent to our service.

### Canvas Content and Drafts

Canvas drafts may contain drawings, text, selected photos, layout settings, and any audio or video you add using available features. These materials are processed on your device to let you edit and preview the canvas.

### Published Canvases and Sharing Information

A published canvas has a unique link. The service keeps the published content, the selected viewing duration, and the timestamps and status needed to enforce the link’s availability. Your sent history records basic publication and status information on your device.

### Technical and Security Information

Our hosting services may process request information such as IP addresses, browser or app information, requested URLs, timestamps, and response or error codes. This information helps deliver the viewer, troubleshoot failures, and prevent abuse. Visiting a preview page or loading the envelope does not start the viewing timer.

2.2 Time Canvas may process the following types of information.

(i) **Canvas Content:** Drawings, text, selected photos, and audio or video you add through available canvas features.

(ii) **Local Drafts and Sent History:** Unpublished canvas content and editing information, as well as basic publication times and status records for sent canvases.

(iii) **Published Content:** The canvas you choose to publish, including its selected media, stored in encrypted form by the sharing service.

(iv) **Sharing and Timing Information:** The canvas and link identifiers, publication time, viewing duration, first opening time, and expiration status.

(v) **App Preferences:** Language and other settings used to provide your selected app experience.

(vi) **Technical and Security Information:** Connection data and request or error information used to deliver, protect, and maintain the App and viewer.

(vii) **Publisher Verification Information:** If required for publishing, limited information used to verify your identity or app installation and protect the service. The type of verification depends on the method presented when you publish.

(viii) **Customer Service and Feedback Information:** Your message and any optional email address or other details you choose to provide when contacting us, including through an available Formspree form.

(ix) **Purchase Information:** If you buy an available Time Canvas Pro upgrade, the applicable app store handles payment. We may process product identifiers, purchase references, and purchase status as needed to confirm, restore, or manage your entitlement.

The app store handles payment credentials. We receive the purchase information necessary to manage the entitlement.

2.3 Time Canvas may use the following device capabilities for features that you choose.

(i) **Photo and File Access:** The App processes photos or supported media that you select for a canvas using your device’s selection controls.

(ii) **Microphone and Camera:** When recording features are available, the App requests the relevant permission when you choose to record audio or video. Recordings remain in the local draft until you publish the canvas containing them.

(iii) **Internet Access:** Internet connectivity is used to publish and view canvases, check link status, and access available purchase, update, or support services.

2.4 The core drawing, text, and photo features use the content you select. Contacts, precise location, and permissions for optional recording features are unnecessary for these core features.

2.5 Publishing a canvas sends the selected content to the sharing service. Other unpublished drafts stay on your device.

2.6 Time Canvas does not automatically sync unpublished canvases to our service or send canvas content to a remote AI service for analysis.

## 3. How Information Is Used

3.1 Time Canvas processes information and data for the following purposes.

(i) Creating, editing, previewing, and saving canvas drafts.

(ii) Adding and arranging selected drawings, text, photos, and supported recordings.

(iii) Publishing a canvas that you choose to share and generating its unique link.

(iv) Showing a preview envelope and starting the shared timer when the first viewer chooses to open the canvas.

(v) Delivering the canvas within the permitted viewing period, blocking expired requests, and handling removal from cloud storage.

(vi) Showing the sender the published canvas’s status and maintaining local sent history.

(vii) Operating and securing publishing and viewing, preventing abuse, and investigating technical failures.

(viii) Receiving and responding to support requests or feedback you choose to submit.

(ix) Confirming, acknowledging, restoring, and managing optional Pro purchases and entitlements.

(x) Checking for app updates and maintaining the App and viewer.

3.2 We do not sell, rent, or trade your canvas content or personal information.

3.3 We do not use private canvas content to build advertising profiles or target advertisements.

3.4 Browser viewing uses the shared link and technical information needed to load the viewer and enforce its access rules. Viewers can use this feature without providing a name or email address.

3.5 A messaging or social platform you choose may fetch a preview of the link. Time Canvas serves a generic envelope on preview requests. The preview does not contain the published canvas or start the viewing period.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Unpublished drafts, app preferences, and basic sent-history records are stored on your device. The time-limited online copy is created when you choose to publish. It is transferred over HTTPS and stored in encrypted form.

4.2 If no one opens a published canvas, its link expires 24 hours after publication for Free or seven days after publication for an eligible Pro canvas.

4.3 The first viewer to select Open Canvas starts the viewing period. The sender chooses a duration from 5 to 30 seconds. All viewers use the same deadline, and opening the link again does not restart the timer.

4.4 After the deadline, the service rejects requests for the canvas and the viewer stops displaying it. The encrypted cloud copy and its associated decryption key are scheduled for removal. Cloud cleanup can happen after access has ended. Limited historical copies may remain for the period allowed by the applicable storage settings.

4.5 We retain limited link status and technical records as needed to enforce expiration, provide status information, prevent abuse, and maintain the service. Support correspondence and purchase information may have separate retention periods based on support, entitlement, and applicable legal needs.

4.6 A viewer may capture, record, or forward material during the viewing period. Our control of access applies to the Time Canvas service. Copies made by viewers or kept by other services remain under their control after expiration.

4.7 You can manage drafts through the available app controls and remove locally stored app information through your device settings, subject to operating system behavior.

4.8 Uninstalling the App affects its local data, subject to operating system storage and backup behavior. A canvas already published continues to follow its assigned online expiration. Original files in your device library are managed separately.

4.9 Sent history is intended to show publication and expiration status. Keep any original materials you want to retain in a location you control.

4.10 We use measures such as encrypted storage and server-enforced access deadlines to protect published content. The security of local drafts also depends on the protections in place on your device.

## 5. Information Sharing and Disclosure

5.1 We deliver a published canvas to holders of its valid link during the permitted viewing period. You choose where to send the link and who receives it through the channels you use.

(ii) **App Stores:** Google Play, and the Apple App Store if applicable, process purchases, payment, refunds, and other store operations under their own terms and privacy policies.

(iii) **Formspree:** If you submit a support message through a Formspree form, Formspree transmits the message and any details you enter to us. The form submission contains the information you choose to enter.

(iv) **User-Initiated Sharing:** The messaging app, social service, or other destination you choose may process the link or generate a preview. Anyone who receives the valid link, including someone it was forwarded to, may be able to open it.

(v) **Website, Telegram, and Update Services:** These services may process ordinary connection data when you open an external page or use an update check. An update check uses version and platform information as needed to determine whether an update is available.

5.3 We may disclose information when required by applicable law, legal process, or a valid request from a competent authority.

5.4 Hosting and support providers may process information in the regions where they operate. The information available through these services is limited by the data you choose to send and the data needed to deliver and maintain the service.

5.5 Sharing a canvas with an outside service or recipient is your choice. After they receive the information, their handling of it is subject to their own practices or obligations.

## 10. Content Sharing and Data Responsibilities

10.1 Share material you own or are otherwise authorized to share.

10.2 Obtain any permissions needed before publishing photos, recordings, or information about other people.

10.3 Review the contents of your canvas before publishing, including any private details visible in photos or audible in recordings.

10.4 Treat a valid canvas link as access to the published canvas. Send it to the people you intend to share it with.

10.5 Choose a sharing channel appropriate for the intended audience. Posting a link in a public channel may make it available beyond your intended recipients.

10.6 Consider the sensitivity of the information before publishing. A short viewing period limits the time for access through Time Canvas.

10.7 If timing matters, coordinate with your recipients before sharing. The first viewer to open the canvas starts the timer for everyone.

10.8 Keep your original materials separately if you need access after the canvas expires.

10.9 Protect your device with the security and access controls available on it.

10.10 Manage original photos and recordings in the storage location where you keep them. The App manages its own draft and published copies.

10.11 If you view a canvas, respect the sender’s privacy when deciding whether to capture, reuse, or forward its contents.

10.12 If a message or link was sent to the wrong audience, use the controls in the sharing service you used to manage that message.

10.13 When you contact support, include only the details needed to explain the request.

10.14 If you suspect unauthorized access or misuse, contact us so we can review the information under our control.
