# Memento (Motivational Time) Privacy Policy
**Last Updated: November 3, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

1.1 Memento (Motivational Time) (hereinafter referred to as "the App") is a personal time-awareness and meaningful-living companion designed to help users focus on important people, turn personal wishes into achievable actions, and preserve meaningful memories.

1.2 This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed, stored, shared, and protected when you use the App. We respect your privacy and aim to minimize the collection of personal information.

1.3 The App is designed with a local-first approach. Personal content is intended to remain on your device unless you choose to export, back up, share, or submit information through a third-party service. Certain optional services, including purchases and customer support, may involve external providers.

1.4 For privacy questions or requests, contact us at contact@kaiqstudio.com.

## 2. Information Processed by the App

### 2.1 Personal Profiles and Time References

The App may process information you voluntarily enter about yourself or important people in your life, including names, nicknames, birthdays, profile images, and user-selected time references.

Time references are intended for reflection and personal planning. They are not predictions of an individual's lifespan.

### 2.2 Wishes, Activities, and Personal Goals

The App may store wishes, personal goals, descriptions, next steps, planned activities, dates, completion status, and associations with selected people.

This information is used to organize meaningful activities and help you revisit your plans.

### 2.3 Memories and Personal Content

You may voluntarily create personal memories containing text, photographs, dates, and associations with people or wishes.

This content is intended to be stored locally unless you explicitly export, back up, or share it.

### 2.4 Preferences and App Settings

The App may process language preferences, appearance settings, reminder preferences, notification settings, and other configuration choices required for its features.

### 2.5 Purchase and Subscription Information

If paid features are available and you choose to purchase them, the applicable app store processes your payment.

The App may receive purchase status, product identifiers, transaction identifiers, and entitlement information necessary to activate or restore paid features.

The App does not require your full payment card number to provide purchase-related features.

If a third-party purchase management provider is integrated, that provider may process technical identifiers and transaction information under its own privacy policy.

### 2.6 Customer Support Information

If you contact us through email or an in-app support form, we may receive the information you voluntarily submit, such as your email address, message content, and relevant technical details.

Please do not include sensitive personal or medical information in support messages.

### 2.7 Technical and Diagnostic Information

The App and its integrated service providers may process limited technical information necessary for functionality, purchases, security, troubleshooting, or service delivery.

Depending on the final implementation, this may include app version, operating system version, device-related identifiers, purchase-related diagnostics, and error information.

Any collection or transmission of diagnostic data must be consistent with the services actually integrated into the released App.

## 3. How Information Is Used

We process information for the following purposes:

1. To display personal time references and important people.
2. To organize wishes, next steps, and planned activities.
3. To save and display memories created by users.
4. To provide optional local notifications and reminders.
5. To maintain user preferences and app settings.
6. To activate and restore paid features when applicable.
7. To respond to customer support requests.
8. To maintain app functionality, reliability, and security.

We do not use personal memories or information about loved ones for behavioral advertising.

## 4. Device Permissions

### 4.1 Notifications

The App may request notification permission to provide reminders about activities, important dates, wishes, or other events selected by the user.

You may disable notifications through the App or your device settings. Disabling notifications does not prevent you from accessing locally stored content.

### 4.2 Photos and Media

The App may allow you to select photographs for profiles or personal memories.

Where supported, the App may use the operating system's photo picker instead of requesting broad access to your photo library.

The App does not require access to unrelated photographs for its core functions.

### 4.3 Files and Storage

The App may use system file selection and sharing interfaces when you export, back up, restore, or share information.

Access is intended to be limited to the files and locations you select, subject to operating system behavior.

## 5. Local Storage and Data Security

5.1 The App follows a local-first design. Personal profiles, wishes, memories, and preferences are intended to be stored on your device.

5.2 Local-first storage does not mean that all network communication is disabled. Purchase verification, customer support, operating system services, and user-selected sharing or backup destinations may involve external communication.

5.3 We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the App. However, no device, storage method, or electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5.4 You are responsible for protecting access to your device and any backup files you create.

5.5 If backup files are not encrypted, anyone who obtains access to those files may be able to read their contents. You should store backups only in locations you trust.

## 6. Backup, Restore, and Sharing

### 6.1 Manual Backup and Restore

The App may provide options to export personal data to a file and restore data from a previously created backup.

Backup files may contain personal profiles, wishes, memories, settings, and other information included in the selected export format.

If you choose a cloud storage provider as the destination, the provider may store and process the backup according to its own terms and privacy policy.

We do not control the security, retention, or availability of files stored with third-party providers.

### 6.2 Sharing

If you choose to share content through your device's native sharing interface, the selected destination application may receive the content you share.

The privacy practices of that destination are governed by its own policies.

The App does not need to identify your social media contacts to provide standard operating system sharing functionality.

## 7. Third-Party Services

### 7.1 App Stores and Payments

Purchases may be processed through Google Play or the Apple App Store, depending on the platform on which the App is distributed.

These platforms may process payment information, transaction records, and technical data under their respective privacy policies.

### 7.2 Purchase Management Providers

If the App integrates a third-party purchase management service, such as RevenueCat, that provider may process purchase-related identifiers, entitlement status, and technical information necessary to validate purchases and subscriptions
