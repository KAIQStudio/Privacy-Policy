# Smart Photo Guide Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: November 15, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

1.1 "Smart Photo Guide" (hereinafter referred to as "this App") is a still photography application designed to help you frame and take photos using on-device image analysis and camera guidance. This App is developed by KAIQStudio ("we", "us", or "our").

1.2 This Privacy Policy describes how the initial Android version of Smart Photo Guide is designed to process, store, protect, and, where applicable, transmit information. Sections describing optional services apply only when those services are available in your version of the App.

1.3 The App requests camera permission when you choose to use its photography features. You can decline or revoke that permission in your device settings.

1.4 If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact us at:

**Email: contact@kaiqstudio.com**

1.5 Smart Photo Guide is designed for still photography. The initial version does not include video or audio recording, and you do not need to create an App account or sign in to use the camera features.


## 2. Information and Data We Process

2.1 Smart Photo Guide is designed to process camera images on your device and to handle live preview frames differently from photos you choose to take.

### Live Camera Preview

While the camera is active, the App can analyze live preview frames on your device to assess framing, lighting, and the position of people or other subjects. Depending on the features available, the App may detect the presence and position of a face or estimate a person's pose to help you compose the photo.

Preview frames are intended for temporary processing in memory. The App does not save them as photos or video, upload them for cloud analysis, or use them to identify people or build facial recognition profiles.

### Captured Photos

When you press the shutter, the App is designed to save the resulting photo to your device's shared photo storage, where you can manage it through your system gallery. Some versions may also check a captured photo on your device for issues such as blur or a subject being cut off.

Smart Photo Guide does not operate a cloud photo library or automatically upload your photos. A gallery, backup service, or sharing app you use may handle those photos separately according to your settings and that service's privacy practices.

2.2 The types of information or data that may be processed when you use Smart Photo Guide include:

(i) **Camera Preview Data:** Live frames and temporary results such as subject position, face or pose estimates, and lighting or composition measurements used to guide a shot.

(ii) **Captured Photos:** Photos you choose to take, including any photo metadata created during saving. The App is not designed to request your location or add GPS coordinates to photos.

(iii) **Device Motion and Orientation Data:** Readings from supported sensors, such as the accelerometer or gyroscope, used on your device to estimate camera tilt and help you level a shot. These readings are not used to determine your geographic location or track your movements outside the camera feature.

(iv) **Customer Service and Feedback Information:** When the support form is available, you can open it from the App and choose to submit a message. The form sends your message and any email address you choose to provide through Formspree so that we can review and respond to it.

(v) **Update Check Information:** When the update service is configured, its HTTPS endpoint may receive limited request details, such as platform or App version information if included in the request, and standard connection information such as your IP address. See Section 7A for details.

2.3 Smart Photo Guide may use certain device capabilities to provide the photography features you choose to use:

(i) **Camera Access:** Used for the live viewfinder, photography guidance, and taking photos. These features require camera access.

(ii) **Photo Storage Access:** Used to save photos you take through the device's media storage. The App is designed to save those photos without browsing your existing photo library or requesting broad access to it.

2.4 The initial version is not designed to request microphone or location access. Photos, live preview frames, and motion or orientation readings are not included in support submissions or update requests sent by the App.


## 3. How Information Is Used

3.1 Smart Photo Guide uses information to provide the following functions:

(i) Displaying the live camera preview while you use the viewfinder;

(ii) Analyzing the scene on your device to suggest adjustments to framing, lighting, subject placement, or camera position when helpful;

(iii) Using available camera controls for exposure, focus, and white balance;

(iv) Using motion and orientation readings to give tilt or level guidance during photography;

(v) Saving photos you choose to take and, where supported, checking them on your device for common photo quality issues;

(vi) Receiving, reviewing, and responding to support or feedback messages you choose to submit;

(vii) Checking whether a newer version of the App is available when the update service is configured;

(viii) Keeping the support and update services secure and troubleshooting problems with those services.

3.2 We do not sell, rent, or trade your photos, live preview frames, or information derived from those images.

3.3 Smart Photo Guide does not use your photos, preview frames, face or pose estimates, or motion readings for advertising, user profiling, facial recognition, or AI model training.


## 4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Photos you take are designed to be saved in your device's shared photo storage. Smart Photo Guide does not maintain its own remote copy of those photos.

4.2 Live preview frames, temporary image analysis results, and camera orientation readings are intended for use during the active camera session. The App does not maintain a permanent history of this data.

4.3 The initial version does not provide its own photo backup, restore, or cloud sync service. Your device or gallery app may back up photos independently if you have enabled that service.

4.4 The App's update service, when configured, uses HTTPS. Support submissions are intended to be sent through an HTTPS form. Please do not include passwords, private photos, or other sensitive information in your message.

4.5 You can delete photos through your system gallery or other device photo controls. Clearing App data or uninstalling Smart Photo Guide does not ordinarily remove photos saved in Android's shared photo storage. If your gallery uses a Trash or Recently Deleted folder, copies may remain there until emptied or automatically removed.

4.6 We generally cannot recover deleted photos because we do not maintain a remote copy. Backed up or shared copies may remain in other services until you remove them through those services.


## 5. Information Sharing and Disclosure

5.1 Smart Photo Guide is not designed to send your photos, live preview frames, image analysis results, or motion readings to us or our service providers. If you share a saved photo from your device or enable backup in another app, that app or service may receive the photo and its metadata.

5.2 Limited information may be processed by third-party services in the following circumstances:

(i) **Formspree:** When you choose to submit the support form, Formspree processes your message and any email address you provide. The form does not automatically attach photos, camera data, device identifiers, or App diagnostics. Formspree may independently receive standard browser and connection information when you use the form.

(ii) **Update Service Hosting:** When an update check is made, the server and its hosting provider may process standard connection details, including your IP address, and limited request information needed to serve the version response. Update requests do not include photos, live preview frames, or sensor readings.

(iii) **External Services:** When you open a website, Google Play listing, or Telegram channel, the destination may process information about your visit under its own privacy practices. The App reads no photo content for its Share App feature, which is designed to share the App description and download link through your system share sheet.

5.3 We may disclose information we hold when required by applicable law, legal process, or a valid request from a competent authority. We generally do not possess the photos or camera data that remain on your device.

5.4 We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.


## 6. User Rights and Choices

6.1 You can control how you use Smart Photo Guide and manage your photos. Depending on your device and the features available, you may:

(i) Choose whether to grant camera access and revoke it in your device settings;

(ii) Choose when to take a photo by pressing the shutter;

(iii) View and manage photos saved to your device through your system gallery;

(iv) Delete saved photos and, if applicable, remove them from your gallery's Trash or Recently Deleted folder;

(v) Choose whether to share photos or enable backup or sync in another app or service;

(vi) Clear locally stored App settings and other App data through your device settings;

(vii) Uninstall Smart Photo Guide without deleting photos saved in shared storage.

6.2 The customer service or feedback form is optional. Your message and optional email address are sent through Formspree only when you submit the form. You may also contact us by email.

6.3 Opening the KAIQStudio Telegram channel or website is optional. A failed or unavailable update check does not prevent you from taking photos.

6.4 If you have privacy-related questions or requests, you may contact us at:

**Email: contact@kaiqstudio.com**

6.5 You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of support information associated with you. Please provide enough information to identify the relevant correspondence, such as the approximate submission date and the email address used, if any. Do not include passwords or private photos. We will review your request and act on information within our control as applicable. Where a service provider controls the relevant records, we will use available controls or explain how to contact that provider.

7.6 We do not control the privacy practices of independent apps and services you choose to use.


## 7A. Retention and Deletion of Support and Technical Data

7A.1 We do not operate a remote photo storage service. Deleting photos, clearing App data, or uninstalling the App does not delete support messages you previously sent or copies held by other services.

7A.2 Support messages and any email address you provide may be retained as long as reasonably needed to respond to your request, follow up on the issue, maintain relevant support records, prevent abuse, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations. Copies may be held in Formspree, where used, and in the email services used to receive and respond to your message. Those providers' settings and retention practices may affect how long copies remain in their systems.

7A.3 To request deletion of support information, email **contact@kaiqstudio.com** with enough detail to identify the message. We will review your request. We may retain information where necessary for legal, security, or dispute resolution reasons, or direct you to the provider for records outside our control.

7A.4 When a valid HTTPS update endpoint is configured, the App is designed to check for an available version once after launch and to allow a manual retry from Settings if available. The server or hosting provider may log standard connection information, such as your IP address and the time of the request. The update check does not require an App account and does not send photos, live preview frames, image analysis results, motion readings, or support messages.

7A.5 Update request logs, if retained, may be used to operate or secure the update service and troubleshoot failures. The retention of any logs held by the hosting provider depends on that provider and the service configuration.
