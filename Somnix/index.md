## 2. Information and Data We Process

2.1 How we handle your information depends on the feature you use.

### Dream Records and On-Device Features

Somnia lets you review and edit the bedtime script and nighttime cue, record what you remember after waking, and configure audio playback. Somnia processes manual edits on your device. If cloud storage or cross-device sync is offered, the records covered by that feature are described in Section 4.2.

### Cloud AI Generation and Text-to-Speech

When you request a new generation or regeneration, Somnia sends the text you submit, your selected mode, language and region, and any relevant optional scene preferences or sound sensitivity setting. One AI request produces a bedtime script, a shorter nighttime cue, and keywords used to revisit the scene. When you choose to continue a dream or use feedback to personalize a generation, Somnia includes only the relevant prior dream or feedback summary with your permission. If cloud Text-to-Speech (TTS) is used for playback, the service receives the text to be spoken, including your edits if you choose to hear the edited version, and the language and voice settings needed to synthesize it.

2.2 The types of information that may be processed when you use Somnia are described below.

(i) Your **Dream Text and Journal Records** may include the scenes, people, emotions, and goals you describe, your original and edited scripts, dream summaries, and keywords or details you choose to carry into a later dream. You can omit names and other identifying details from text you submit for cloud processing.

(ii) Your **Wake-Up Feedback and Playback Settings** may include whether you remember the dream, whether it felt close to your chosen theme, whether you heard a cue or woke up because of it, whether you want to continue the dream, and any optional notes. Playback settings may include your intended bedtime, optional wake time, estimated time to fall asleep, selected cue time, sound sensitivity, and audio preferences.

(iii) Your **Account and Dream Credits Information** may include an account identifier, the sign-in information needed to authenticate you, and records of your Dream Credits balance, use, and reset dates. (v) Your **Subscription Information** may include the product purchased, a purchase token or other transaction reference, and entitlement, renewal, expiry, or refund status made available by Google Play. We use this information to verify, manage, and restore access. Google Play handles your payment details.

## 3. How Information Is Used

3.1 Somnia uses information for the following purposes.

(i) Creating personalized bedtime scripts, nighttime cues, and recall keywords when you request a generation.

(ii) Saving and displaying dream records, original and edited scripts, and wake-up notes you choose to keep.

(iii) Synthesizing speech, letting you preview the audio, and playing cues using your chosen time and sound settings.

(iv) Using the prior dream or wake-up feedback summary you agree to include to continue a dream or personalize a new generation. The App can also use your feedback to suggest adjustments to your playback settings that you can change yourself.

(v) Providing account access, administering Dream Credits, and making supported information available for export or deletion.

(vi) Receiving and responding to support messages and privacy requests.

(vii) Verifying and restoring your Google Play subscription and updating your access as its status changes.

(viii) Checking for optional updates, troubleshooting service errors, enforcing usage limits, and protecting the App and its users from unauthorized access or abuse.

3.2 We prohibit the sale, rental, or trade of your personal information.

3.3 We limit our use of your private dream content to the purposes described in Section 3.1. The handling of prompts and outputs by cloud providers, including any retention or use for model training, is addressed in Section 7A.4.
