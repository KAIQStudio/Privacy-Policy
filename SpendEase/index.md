# SpendEase Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: November 4, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

1.1 "SpendEase" (hereinafter referred to as "this App") is a personal spending management application designed to help users record selected expenses, organize spending categories, plan budgets, monitor available spending amounts, and understand spending patterns over time. We are committed to protecting user privacy and minimizing the collection and processing of personal information.

1.2 This Privacy Policy explains how SpendEase processes, stores, protects, and, where applicable, transmits information when you use the App.

1.3 By using this App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this Policy, please discontinue use of the App.

1.4 If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact us at:

**Email: [contact@kaiqstudio.com](mailto:contact@kaiqstudio.com)**

1.5 SpendEase is intended for personal spending organization and budget planning. It is not a banking service, financial institution, investment platform, or professional financial advisory service.

1.6 SpendEase does not require users to register an account, connect a bank account, or provide income, assets, liabilities, or payment card information to use its core spending management features.

## 2. Information and Data We Process

2.1 SpendEase follows the principles of data minimization, local-first processing, and privacy by design. Personal spending records and budget information are designed to remain on the user's device unless the user explicitly chooses an export, backup, or external sharing action.

### Spending Records

SpendEase allows users to record expenses that they personally choose to manage. These records are stored locally on the user's device and are used to calculate spending totals, remaining budgets, daily spending allowances, and historical comparisons.

### Budget and Category Information

SpendEase allows users to create and manage spending categories, define budget periods, set spending limits, and adjust budgets according to their preferences.

Budget periods may be configured using monthly subdivisions or weekly cycles. This information is processed locally to provide consistent calculations across the App.

### Spending Analysis and Reports

SpendEase processes locally stored spending and budget information to generate summaries, charts, spending trends, budget comparisons, and historical reports.

These calculations are intended to help users understand their own spending patterns. They are not used by us for advertising profiling or the sale of personal financial information.

2.2 The types of information or data that may be processed when you use SpendEase include:

(i) **Spending Records:** Expense amounts, selected spending categories, transaction dates, and related record modification information.

(ii) **Spending Categories:** Category names, icons, display order, creation information, and active or archived status.

(iii) **Budget Information:** User-defined spending limits, budget periods, adjustment percentages, budget revisions, and related planning information.

(iv) **Spending Statistics:** Calculated spending totals, remaining budgets, average daily spending allowances, historical comparisons, and chart data.

(v) **App Preferences:** Language preferences, display settings, selected categories, and other settings necessary to personalize the App experience.

(vi) **Home Screen Widget Information:** The selected spending category and calculated budget information necessary to display the App's home screen widget.

(vii) **Backup and Export Data:** Spending records, categories, budgets, and supported settings that users intentionally export or restore using the App's backup, restore, or CSV export functions.

(viii) **Customer Service and Feedback Information:** If you choose to submit a customer service inquiry or feedback through a Formspree-powered form, the information you intentionally provide, including your message and any contact details entered, is transmitted through Formspree so that we can receive and respond to your request.

(ix) **Purchase Information:** If you choose to purchase the optional SpendEase Pro upgrade, Google Play processes the payment transaction on Android. SpendEase may receive purchase-related information necessary to verify, acknowledge, restore, or manage your Pro entitlement.

We do not directly collect or store your payment card number, banking credentials, or Google account password.

2.3 SpendEase may use certain device capabilities when necessary to provide features that you actively choose to use:

(i) **File Access:** SpendEase may access files that you explicitly select or create when exporting spending records, creating a backup, or restoring previously exported data.

(ii) **Home Screen Widgets:** SpendEase may provide selected spending and budget information to its home screen widget through supported operating system mechanisms.

(iii) **Internet Access:** Internet connectivity may be used for purchase-related operations, checking for App updates, accessing external websites, and opening customer service or feedback services.

2.4 SpendEase does not require access to your bank accounts, credit card statements, contacts, microphone, camera, or precise location for its core spending management functions.

2.5 SpendEase does not automatically transmit your locally stored spending records, budgets, categories, or historical spending reports to servers operated by us.

2.6 SpendEase does not provide automatic bank synchronization, automatic cloud synchronization of personal spending records, or an AI service that uploads spending histories for remote analysis.

## 3. How Information Is Used

3.1 Information and data processed by SpendEase are used only as necessary to provide the App's functions, including:

(i) Recording, displaying, modifying, and deleting expenses that you choose to manage;

(ii) Creating, editing, organizing, and archiving spending categories;

(iii) Setting and adjusting spending budgets and budget periods;

(iv) Calculating spending totals, remaining budgets, remaining days, and average daily spending allowances;

(v) Providing spending trends, budget comparisons, historical statistics, and visual reports;

(vi) Displaying selected spending and budget information through home screen widgets;

(vii) Creating user-requested backups, restoring supported data, and exporting spending information;

(viii) Receiving and responding to customer service or feedback messages that you intentionally submit through Formspree;

(ix) Verifying, acknowledging, managing, and restoring SpendEase Pro purchase entitlements through the applicable app store;

(x) Checking for available App updates and maintaining compatibility, security, and proper operation of the App.

3.2 We do not sell, rent, or trade your spending records, budget information, category information, or locally generated financial summaries to third parties.

3.3 SpendEase does not use the contents of your locally stored spending records for behavioral advertising, advertising profiling, or third-party marketing.

3.4 SpendEase does not require users to provide complete financial information or disclose their personal income to receive its core budgeting and spending management functions.

3.5 Spending statistics and budget suggestions are generated from the information that users choose to record. These results are intended for personal reference and do not represent verified information about a user's complete financial circumstances.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Spending records, categories, budgets, App preferences, and related historical information are designed to be stored locally on your device.

4.2 SpendEase does not operate a remote cloud account service for automatically storing or synchronizing users' personal spending records.

4.3 SpendEase provides user-initiated backup, restore, and export functions for supported information. When you export data, the resulting files are placed under your control through the supported device or file selection mechanisms.

4.4 Exported backup and CSV files may contain personal spending information. The confidentiality of these files depends on the applicable export format, storage location, device protections, and any additional security measures used by the user.

4.5 Exported files are not automatically uploaded to servers operated by us. If you choose to save or share an exported file through a third-party storage provider, cloud service, messaging application, or other external destination, that provider's privacy and security practices may apply.

4.6 You are responsible for selecting an appropriate storage location for exported files and taking reasonable precautions to prevent unauthorized access.

4.7 You may modify or delete individual spending records through available App functions. You may also remove locally stored App information through your device settings, subject to the behavior of the operating system.

4.8 Uninstalling SpendEase may remove locally stored spending records, budgets, categories, and related App data. Exported files stored outside the App may remain available until separately deleted.

4.9 Because SpendEase does not maintain a remote copy of your personal spending records, information permanently deleted from your device without an available usable backup may not be recoverable.

4.10 No software, storage method, or security mechanism can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their devices, exported files, and any external storage accounts they choose to use.
